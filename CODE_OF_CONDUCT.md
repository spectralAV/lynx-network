# Code Of Conduct

Lynx Network (`lynx-network`) should be a practical and respectful place for live audio engineers, RF technicians, and developers to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear, patient, and constructive.
- Respect different experience levels across live sound engineering, RF coordination, and software development.
- Keep feedback focused on technical accuracy, show reliability, and code quality.
- Credit hardware protocols, upstream libraries, and third-party projects accurately.
- Avoid sharing private venue network topologies, credentials, or unlicensed proprietary firmware files.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private venue or client information without consent.
- Spam, repeated off-topic comments, or bad-faith issue reports.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments and issues that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may result in blocked participation.
